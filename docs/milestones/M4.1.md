## Touchpad / Mouse

Local review of Pi/TUI **1.4.0**:
`dist/tui.d.ts:TuiMouseEvent` offers `wheelDelta?: number`, no deltaX/axis.
`dist/tui-alt-screen.js:parseWheelEvent` accepts only directions 0/1 (SGR 64/65),
rejects 2/3 (66/67) as wheel. Current adapter adds no horizontal axis.

Therefore **no horizontal touchpad support** in this release. Horizontality not inferred
from Shift+wheel nor global sequences/Kitty protocols intercepted.
Physical gestures depend on host terminal; translation not promised.
Vertical wheel, selection capture, and divider resize maintain prior routing.
Use keyboard with focus on Diff. Herdr integration or states not modified.
